# Promote & Lead instructor coaching guidance

Each of the 87 supplied discussion sections now has a separate coaching object: main idea, two listening indicators, a unique illustrative response, two follow-up questions, and a common misunderstanding. The first follow-up is specific to the discussion; the second invites reflection on what to repeat or change.

These notes are interpretations for facilitation, not official RBLP answers, grading criteria, or a certification-readiness judgment. Each expandable panel identifies that distinction and encourages learners to use their own experience. The original readings, examples, questions, source metadata, and track information are unchanged.

Notes appear directly beneath the discussion questions in the instructor teaching panel. Their open/closed preference persists with the local browser key `pl-proposal-coaching-open`, is cleared by Reset sample changes, and is excluded from print. This is a UI preference, not an authorization boundary; the preview remains an unauthenticated local prototype. Existing next/back navigation and completion behavior are preserved.

Validation: 87 complete note sets with unique illustrative responses; exact comparison confirms the original curriculum content is unchanged after excluding the added coaching fields; JavaScript syntax; all 87 next actions and ten principal routes; open-preference persistence; backward navigation, completion, and unfinished recovery. Browser visual testing was not performed. No deployment or backend changes.
