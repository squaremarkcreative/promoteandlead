## Crisp generation prompt

Create a transparent-background PNG website hero illustration, crisp faceted brand-emblem style. Subject: exactly three forest-green mountain peaks, tallest in center, smaller flanking peaks, a single ivory winding path ascending center mountain to its summit, and a dimensional metallic gold five-point star immediately above the summit. Inspired by Promote & Lead's mountain/path/star motif. Angular geometric clean silhouette, elegant precise planes of deep green #155124 and forest #214e40 with restrained sage highlights, gold #d6a735 star. Flat graphic precision with subtle faceted depth, professional leadership academy brand. No words or typography. ISOLATED CUTOUT with genuinely transparent alpha background, no white or ivory background, no rectangle, no studio surface, no cast shadow outside the silhouette, no glow, no pedestal. Tight composition with entire subject visible and only 3 percent transparent margin on all sides. Broad mountain base, balanced centered composition. Ivory exists ONLY within the winding path. Do not include any wordmark.

## Sculpted generation prompt

Generate a second, softer SCULPTED version of a leadership brand's mountain emblem as a PNG with genuinely transparent alpha background. Three simple forest-green triangular mountains, tall central peak, two smaller side peaks; an ivory winding path climbs the front central mountain; one faceted five-point gold star sits immediately above the summit. Elegant matte ceramic / folded architectural paper sculpture with soft bevels on the edges, broad simple planes (not lots of jagged rocks), forest green #214e40 with sage-toned highlights and brushed warm gold star. Keep precisely three peaks and one star. The silhouette is compact and broad, shaped like a brand logo. Studio-quality material details and soft shading ONLY INSIDE the objects. Background must be completely transparent, alpha zero everywhere outside mountain and star: NO glow or haze, NO surface or floor, NO rectangular backdrop, NO outside drop shadow, NO reflected light around base. No text. Entire shape tightly framed with 3 percent margin, all peaks and star visible. Wide 3:2 composition.
